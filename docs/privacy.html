AUX

Privacy Policy

Effective November 9, 2026

AUX is an iOS app for ranking music, built by a student team as a capstone project at California State University, Fullerton. This policy explains what AUX collects, who can see it, and how to delete it. Questions go to auxappmobile@gmail.com.

What we collect

  • Account details: your email address, username and password. We never store your password itself, only a one-way scrambled version (a hash) that can't be turned back into it.
  • Profile: your bio and, if you add one, a profile photo. When you pick a photo, the app only receives that one photo.
  • Music activity: the songs you rank, your scores, their order, and when you ranked them. When you import a playlist, AUX reads its list of songs to fill your rank queue; it doesn't save the playlist link.
  • Your rank queue (songs waiting to be ranked) is stored on your phone, not on our servers.
  • Social activity: friend requests and friendships, people you block, and reports you send.
  • Security information: the IP address of login and signup attempts, held briefly in memory to stop password guessing. Our hosting provider also keeps standard server logs.

What we don't do

  • No ads, no tracking across other apps or websites, and no analytics tools.
  • We don't sell your data or share it with advertisers or data brokers.
  • AUX doesn't sign in to your Apple Music or Spotify account. Search, charts and previews come from Apple's public music catalog.

Who can see what

  • Anyone with an AUX account can see your username, bio, profile photo, when you joined, how many songs you've ranked, and your rankings.
  • Friends also see your score on each song's page, and anyone can see a taste-match percentage with you once you've ranked enough of the same songs.
  • Your scores count toward the AUX Hot 100 without your name attached.
  • Your email address is never shown to other users.
  • If you block someone, or they block you, you can't see each other's profiles or find each other in search.

Services we use

  • Render hosts our server, and Amazon Web Services stores our database.
  • Apple Music provides song search, charts, artwork and 30-second previews. MusicBrainz is a backup for search. Your searches reach them through our server, without your account details.
  • Spotify is contacted only when you import a Spotify playlist link, to read that public playlist.
  • Apple TestFlight, if you're a beta tester, may send us crash reports and any feedback you choose to submit.

Keeping and deleting your data

  • We keep your data while you have an account.
  • You can delete your account at any time in the app: Profile → Profile settings → Delete account. This immediately removes your account, profile, photo, rankings, friendships, blocks, and reports made by or about you from our database. Copies may remain in database backups for a short time until they expire.
  • To ask about the data we hold about you, email us.

Security

Everything between the app and our server is encrypted (HTTPS), and so is the connection between our server and our database. Your login stays signed in through a token stored in your iPhone's Keychain.

Children

AUX isn't meant for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their data. If you think a child under 13 has an account, email us and we'll delete it.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date at the top.
